One sentence, 200 characters at most: a small moment of wonder in everyday life.

> The microwave beeps and the dog arrives before the sound does, as if he heard the future.

`main.ts` writes that sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (89 characters, no trailing newline) and read-back-verifies it.
